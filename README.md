# Scriptura & Enarratio

The Douay-Rheims Bible with the commentary of Bl. Denis the Carthusian (1402–1471), translated from
the Latin of his *Opera Omnia* (Montreuil, 1896–).

- Choose a book and chapter; tap a verse, or a verse tag in the commentary, to see just that passage
  with the comments on it.
- Commentary can be read in English or in Denis's Latin.

Sources: Douay-Rheims (Challoner revision), public domain. Latin text of Denis from the Montreuil edition,
public domain. The English translation of the commentary was made for this project.

Working notes for editing the site are in `CLAUDE.md`.
